# AURA TFM final — template distillation and controlled deviations

## Template source and authority

- Content baseline: `Definitiva_Entrega_TFM_AURA_Borrador_Final_Ampliado.docx`.
- Accepted-delivery visual reference: `Tercera_Entrega_TFM_Joseph_Gari_AURA.docx`.
- Higher authority: the institutional TFM guide, instructions, regulations, and rubric stored in this folder.
- The final artifact is therefore a standards-compliant reconstruction, not a pixel-identical copy of the draft.

## Page geometry

- A4 portrait.
- Margins: 3.0 cm left, 2.0 cm right, 2.5 cm top, 2.5 cm bottom.
- Header distance: 1.1 cm. Footer distance: 1.1 cm.
- Header: full student name and full TFM title on every body page.
- Footer: centred automatic page number.

## Typography

- Body: Calibri 12 pt, justified, 1.5 line spacing, 6 pt before and after, no first-line indent.
- Heading 1: Calibri Light 18 pt, UNIR blue, page break before major chapters.
- Heading 2: Calibri Light 14 pt, UNIR blue.
- Heading 3: Calibri 12 pt, bold, dark blue.
- Captions: Calibri 10 pt, bold label, placed above the figure or table.
- Sources/notes: Calibri 9 pt, italic, placed below the figure or table.
- Code and hashes: JetBrains Mono or Consolas 8.5–9 pt.

## Colour system

- Primary blue: `#005A8B`.
- Dark ink: `#1F2937`.
- Secondary text: `#5B6573`.
- Light table fill: `#EAF2F7`.
- Evidence green: `#0F766E`.
- Warning amber: `#B7791F`.
- Failure red: `#B42318`.

## Front matter

1. Institutional cover.
2. Declaration of authorship and use of AI-assisted tools.
3. Resumen with 150–300 words and exactly five keywords.
4. Abstract with 150–300 words and exactly five keywords.
5. Automatic table of contents.
6. Automatic list of figures.
7. Automatic list of tables.

## Heading hierarchy and numbering

- Chapters 1–10 use Heading 1 and decimal numbering.
- Subsections use Heading 2 and Heading 3 with consistent decimal numbering.
- Annexes use Heading 1 with letters A–F.
- No manual table-of-contents page is retained.

## Tables

- Caption above; source or methodological note below.
- Header row uses primary blue with white text.
- Body rows alternate white and light blue.
- Repeat header row across pages.
- Avoid split rows where possible.
- Numeric fields are right-aligned; identifiers and prose remain left-aligned.

## Figures and screenshots

- Caption above; source below.
- Screenshots are cropped to the relevant interface area and sized to the text column.
- Academic charts are regenerated from the exported campaign artefacts; they are not inferred from screenshots.
- Use current AURA captures from 14 July 2026 and the definitive second campaign.
- Do not include obsolete screens, old model names, or screens that still describe pending functionality.

## References and citations

- APA 7 author–date citations in the body.
- References favour standards, regulations, official documentation, peer-reviewed papers, and primary technical specifications.
- URLs are included only when required for standards, legislation, software documentation, or datasets.
- No professor-feedback quotations or unverifiable claims are retained.

## Controlled deviations from the source draft

- US Letter is replaced by A4.
- Draft margins and single-spaced body text are replaced by the institutional geometry and 1.5 spacing.
- Manual indices are replaced by Word fields.
- The old `Tercera entrega` header is removed.
- Pending placeholders, future-tense claims, and the extended-third-delivery framing are removed.
- The experimental section is rebuilt around the pilot campaign and the definitive second campaign.
- A transparent AI-tool-use declaration is added because the author reports using coding and writing assistants; no director authorisation is claimed.

## Verification contract

- Render the final DOCX to PDF and PNG.
- Inspect every page for clipping, overflow, blank pages, orphan headings, table splits, caption placement, and screenshot legibility.
- Verify the countable body is at least 60 pages excluding cover, indices, references, and annexes; target a safety margin above that minimum.
- Run package, section, style, table, figure, field, and overflow audits before delivery.
